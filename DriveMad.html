About Drive Mad

Drive Mad is a physics-based driving game where you have to carefully control your vehicle across challenging obstacle courses. Keep your vehicle balanced and reach the finish line without crashing.

Genre
Racing / Physics
Platform
Browser
Type
Driving
Players
Single Player

Controls

  • W / Arrow Up — Accelerate
  • S / Arrow Down — Brake
  • A / Arrow Left — Balance
  • D / Arrow Right — Balance
