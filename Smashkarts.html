About Smash Karts

Smash Karts is a fast-paced multiplayer kart battle game. Race around the track, collect mystery boxes, grab weapons, and use them to smash your opponents.

Controls

  • A / Arrow Left — Turn Left
  • D / Arrow Right — Turn Right
  • Space — Use Item
