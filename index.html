✦ DEVELOPERS

Become a Student Developer

Create games, share your ideas, and become part of the Browser Games community.

1. Create Your Game

Build your game using tools like HTML, JavaScript, Scratch, Python, or any other technology you enjoy. Focus on creativity, learning, and making something fun.

2. Prepare Your Submission

Before submitting your game, include:

• Game title
• Description
• Developer name
• Screenshots
• Controls
• Version number

3. Share Your Game

After your game is reviewed, it can appear on Browser Games for other students to discover and play.
