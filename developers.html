Become a Developer

Build something creative, share your game, and introduce your work to the Browser Games community.

How It Works

Submitting a game to Browser Games is simple. Create your game, prepare the information, send your submission, and wait for review.

1

Create Your Game

Build a game using Scratch, HTML, JavaScript, Python, or another technology you enjoy. Focus on making something fun and creative.

2

Prepare Your Game

Make sure your game works correctly and can be played by other people. Test your controls, links, loading, and overall experience.

3

Prepare Your Information

Choose a title, write a description, select a genre, provide your developer name, and prepare a screenshot or thumbnail.

4

Submit Your Game

Use the submission page to send your game information. Your submission will be reviewed before it appears on Browser Games.

Submission Requirements

Have these things ready before submitting your game.

Game Information

  • Game title
  • Game description
  • Developer name
  • Game genre
  • Version number

Game Details

  • Game URL
  • Controls
  • Platform
  • Player count
  • Difficulty

Media

  • Game thumbnail
  • Game screenshot
  • Optional additional screenshots

Ready to Submit?

Once your game is ready, send us the details through the submission page. We will review the submission before publishing it.
